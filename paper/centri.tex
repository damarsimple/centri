\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{Centri}, an end-to-end system that turns an ordinary
smartphone video of an everyday object moving in a circle into authentic,
difficulty-tiered physics learning material. Unlike prior authentic-problem
generators that couple a phone inertial sensor to the object, Centri measures
the motion \emph{from the video itself}: a vision--language model and a
point-tracking model localise a marked point across frames, and a frozen,
deterministic kinematics pipeline fits the orbit and recovers the angular
kinematics (radius, angular velocity, centripetal acceleration, period,
frequency, and---when the clip captures a spin-up---a constant angular
acceleration). From the resulting measurement \emph{seed}, a local large
language model writes three parallel passages at increasing cognitive
demand---basic, intermediate, and advanced---each grounded strictly in the
measured numbers and aligned to Bloom's taxonomy and Cognitive Load Theory. A
deterministic \emph{grounding gate} verifies every passage (arithmetic
closure, number grounding, tier compliance, motion faithfulness, vocabulary
fences, and cross-tier distinctness) and flags rather than hides violations.
Against a five-object evaluation set the generator achieves monotonic
cognitive-load progression and title consistency on all five objects and a
clean cross-tier verdict on four of five, with the residual issues traced to a
documented self-correction limitation of the local model. We describe the full
pipeline, the evaluation methodology (intrinsic gate, reference-based
similarity, and LLM-as-judge), and the limitations that bound the current
result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% ===========================================================================
Math and physics word problems anchor abstract relations in a concrete
situation; they are most effective when that situation is \emph{authentic}---a
real object the learner can recognise, doing something the learner has
seen~\cite{placeholder}. Authoring authentic problems by hand is slow, and
automating it is hard because the generated text must be simultaneously
correct, solvable, contextually grounded, and pitched at the right difficulty.

Centri targets one physics domain---uniform and non-uniform circular
motion---and makes three choices that distinguish it from prior authentic-%
problem generators in the same lab lineage:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Measurement from video, not from a bolted-on sensor.} The
  kinematics come from computer-vision tracking of the object in an ordinary
  video, so nothing has to be attached to the object and the measured motion is
  the \emph{real} object's motion.
  \item \textbf{A frozen, deterministic measurement core.} The numbers are
  produced by a seeded pipeline, not by the language model, so the same video
  yields byte-identical physics on every run.
  \item \textbf{Difficulty as a first-class output.} The system emits three
  parallel tiers from a single measurement, each gated for grounding and for
  distinctness from the others.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.} (i) An end-to-end pipeline from smartphone
video to grounded, difficulty-tiered material; (ii) a reusable deterministic
\emph{grounding gate} for LLM-generated physics prose; (iii) a multi-method
evaluation (intrinsic gate, reference-based similarity, LLM-as-judge) and an
honest account of where a small local model limits the result.

\section{System Overview}
% ===========================================================================
Centri is three cooperating systems (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}): a
\emph{measurement} backend (video $\rightarrow$ physics), a
\emph{material-generation} stage (physics $\rightarrow$ tiered prose and
figures), and a mobile \emph{app} for capture, human-in-the-loop annotation,
and display. A job runs one video end-to-end: perception localises the object,
the frozen pipeline computes the kinematics and writes a measurement seed, the
generator writes and gates three tiers, and the app renders them.

The measurement and pedagogy stages meet at a single, deliberately narrow
seam---a per-frame CSV of \texttt{time\_s, omega\_rad\_s, ac\_m\_s2}---so the
video output is a drop-in for downstream interactive tutoring.

  spin-up $\alpha$) $\rightarrow$
  \texttt{material\_seed.json} $\rightarrow$

\section{Measurement: Video to Physics}
% ===========================================================================
\subsection{Capture protocol}
Two failure modes are fixed at capture time rather than in software.
\emph{Oblique capture} projects the circular orbit into an ellipse, injecting
a viewing-angle ripple into the per-instant angular velocity that is a
projection artifact, not physics; the pipeline detects this and degrades
gracefully (\texttt{measurement\_quality.reliable = false}, dropping
per-instant claims), but a face-on clip avoids it. \emph{Missing scale} leaves
only scale-free quantities recoverable. The protocol therefore asks for a
face-on, braced camera; a small high-contrast marker (red tape) described by a
simple noun for reliable tracking; a scale reference of known size in frame;
and a \SIrange{10}{15}{\second} clip that captures start~$\rightarrow$~spin-up~%
$\rightarrow$~steady, since the constant-$\alpha$ spin-up is genuine physics
for the advanced tier.

\subsection{Perception}
A vision--language model proposes the object and a bounding region from a
simple-noun prompt; a point-tracking model then follows a marked point across
all frames. A coverage gate retries or, failing that, routes the clip to
human-in-the-loop annotation in the app. Perception is the only place learned
models touch the measurement.

\subsection{Frozen, deterministic kinematics}
The kinematics is \emph{not} authored by the language model. It lives in a
seeded package copied into every job workspace, so ``same video $\rightarrow$
byte-identical physics'' holds by construction. Tracked points are fit to a
circle with a seeded-RNG RANSAC; a single canonical $\omega$ source feeds all
downstream quantities (eliminating an earlier class of prose/table
contradictions); an off-axis orbit centre is recovered rather than assumed;
and when the clip contains a spin-up the pipeline fits a constant angular
acceleration $\alpha$ with its $R^2$. The seed stores unsigned $\omega$ and
$v$ (killing sign contradictions between prose and table), radius $r$, period
$T$, frequency $f$, centripetal acceleration $a_c$, a time-anchored
$\omega(t)$ timeline, and---for the basic tier---angle-at-time milestones
(first crossings of \ang{90}, \ang{180}, \ang{270}, \ang{360}).

\subsection{Accuracy}
An automated ground-truth harness compares the video-only kinematics against a
phone-gyroscope reference. On pilot clips the tracking-only pipeline matches
the gyroscope mean $\omega$ to within \textbf{\SIrange{0.2}{0.3}{\percent}}.
We frame the validity claim as \emph{relative/structural} (trend, peak, phase
shape) rather than absolute calibration against a single phone, because IMU
readings themselves vary with sensor placement and manufacturer; a
known-RPM turntable gives an absolute reference when needed.

\section{Difficulty-Tiered Material Generation}
% ===========================================================================
From the seed alone---never re-deriving quantities---a local LLM
(\texttt{Qwen3.6-35B}, reasoning enabled) writes three passages.

\paragraph{Shared narrative frame.} A single 5W+1H frame (who/what/where/%
when/why/how) is generated once per job; its \texttt{scene\_title} hard-%
overwrites all three tiers, so the tiers can no longer each invent a generic
title (an observed drift failure). If a learner note is present it is
authoritative; otherwise the frame is written in the second person and invents
no proper nouns.

\paragraph{The three tiers} follow a fixed depth ladder grounded in Bloom's
taxonomy and Cognitive Load Theory (Table~\ref{tab:tiers}). Each tier is
assigned a \emph{distinct} worked instant so advanced no longer reads like
intermediate, and each is written in one LLM call (an A3B model bleeds
vocabulary between tiers if asked for all three at once).

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
    \toprule
    Tier & Bloom objective & Cognitive load & Content \\
    \midrule
    Basic        & Remember / Understand & low      & idea + radius + angle-at-time milestones; no equations \\
    Intermediate & Apply / Analyze       & moderate & core relations incl.\ $a_c=\omega^2 r$ on this object's numbers \\
    Advanced     & Analyze / Evaluate    & high     & angular acceleration + timeline + squared-sensitivity + scale caveat \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{The difficulty ladder. Element interactivity rises strictly across
  tiers and is checked by the cross-tier gate.}
  \label{tab:tiers}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Figures are tiered too.} The basic tier renders against three
low-load figures---a labelled still, an angle-at-time coloured-dot plot
(drawn from the same milestones the prose narrates), and the traced
trajectory---while higher tiers use the $\omega(t)$ and $a_c(t)$ series. All
figures are produced by seeded, deterministic plotting code.

\paragraph{Steering a small model.} The local model drafts well but corrects
poorly (Section~\ref{sec:limits}). Two mechanisms address this: (i) an
editable \emph{hints file} of subtle wording nuances, injected into the prompt
as a ``nuances'' block, that steers the \emph{first} pass (e.g.\ say a computed
value ``matches the \emph{measured} value'', never the ``recorded'' value; name
figures by what they show; say a slowing motion ``slows down'' rather than the
steady-sounding ``does not speed up or slow down''); and (ii) a best-of-$N$
failure-informed regeneration that, on a gate failure, re-rolls up to twice fed
the exact faults and keeps only a strictly-fewer-issues draft.

\section{The Grounding Gate}
% ===========================================================================
Every tier passes through a single deterministic gate
(Table~\ref{tab:gate}); a failing tier is regenerated once and then
\emph{kept-but-flagged} (proceed-but-flag---the pipeline never silently ships a
wrong number, and never hard-blocks on prose). The gate verdict, with a
per-job \texttt{frame} block and a \texttt{cross\_tier} block, is written to
\texttt{material\_tiers\_gate.json} and surfaced to the reader.

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
    \toprule
    Check & What it enforces \\
    \midrule
    Arithmetic closure   & every explicit $A\,\mathrm{op}\,B=C$ actually computes \\
    Number grounding     & every quantity traces to the seed (incl.\ the sanctioned $\omega^2$ intermediate) \\
    Tier compliance      & basic has no equations; intermediate/advanced use the assigned relations \\
    Motion faithfulness  & an accelerating/decelerating clip is never called ``steady'' \\
    Vocabulary fences     & no tracking/pipeline jargon; kinematics-only (no forces/causes) \\
    Story fence          & the frame's invented who/where colours only the Scenario section \\
    Cross-tier           & equal titles, distinct worked instants, strictly rising element interactivity \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{The deterministic grounding gate. Idiomatic false positives (``on
  track'', ``work together'') are guarded; correct derived intermediates
  ($\omega^2$) are grounded.}
  \label{tab:gate}
\end{table}

\section{Evaluation Methodology}
% ===========================================================================
We assess the material along three complementary axes, mirroring the
established practice in this research line.

\paragraph{Intrinsic (the gate).} The deterministic gate is itself a quality
signal: per-tier pass/flag, plus cross-tier monotonicity, title equality, and
worked-instant distinctness. It is cheap, reproducible, and pedagogy-aware.

\paragraph{Reference-based similarity.} BLEU and ROUGE ($n$-gram overlap) and
\textbf{BERTScore} (contextual-embedding F1) against a hand-built reference
set. Because a single fixed reference measures lexical density rather than
pedagogical quality (an equation-dense reference scores the \emph{advanced}
tier highest---a confound, not a finding), we sort a 19-source reference set
into basic/intermediate/advanced buckets on the same element-interactivity axis
the material is scored on, and evaluate each tier against its matched bucket.

\paragraph{LLM-as-judge.} A rubric-scored judgment (relevance, answerability,
difficulty-fit, concept accuracy, annotation accuracy) plus an achieved-Bloom
rating, complementing the surface metrics with a semantics-aware signal. A
teacher-rater study on the same rubric is future work; agreement between
human and LLM raters will be reported via correlation / ICC.

\section{Results}
% ===========================================================================
\paragraph{Live generation.} Regenerating a five-object evaluation set
(turntable/phone, bicycle tire, fan) through the reworked generator on the
local model yields (Table~\ref{tab:results}): monotonic element interactivity
on \textbf{5/5} objects, equal titles across tiers on \textbf{5/5}, a clean
cross-tier verdict on \textbf{4/5}, and all three tiers fully clean on
\textbf{3/5} (13/15 tier passages). Residual failures were stochastic
single-word vocabulary flags, addressed by the hints mechanism
(Section~\ref{sec:limits}). The three original defects the rework targeted---%
title drift, degenerate ``\emph{X} on \emph{X}'' naming, and advanced~$\approx$~%
intermediate---were eliminated.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
% ===========================================================================
\paragraph{Local-model self-correction.} The local \texttt{Qwen3.6-35B} drafts
well but self-corrects \emph{poorly}: told that a draft used a banned word, it
frequently re-emits the same word or trades it for another violation (we
observed ``recorded''$\rightarrow$``pushes''). Our mitigation steers the
\emph{first} pass through an editable hints file rather than relying on
regeneration; this eliminated the most stubborn case (the word ``recorded''
went to zero occurrences after one hint). Fully generalising this---a clean
gate on every stochastic draft---likely needs a stronger model or a
review-and-select generation loop (generate several candidates, review, and
choose one). We report this as a genuine bound on the current system, not a
solved problem.

\paragraph{Single-reference confound.} A fixed gold reference measures lexical
overlap, not pedagogy; the tier-matched reference design mitigates but does not
fully remove this.

\paragraph{Absolute scale.} Absolute SI values require a scale reference in
frame; without one only scale-free quantities ($\omega$, $\alpha$, $T$, ratios)
survive. We therefore claim relative/structural validity for the kinematics.

\paragraph{Scope.} The system currently addresses single-object circular
motion; multi-object comparison (e.g.\ pedal vs.\ wheel) and force-based
extensions are future work.

% ===========================================================================
\section{Conclusion}
% ===========================================================================
Centri shows that a smartphone video of an everyday circular motion can be
turned, end-to-end and largely automatically, into authentic physics material
at three grounded difficulty levels---measuring the motion from the video
itself, keeping the physics deterministic, and gating every generated passage
for grounding and tier distinctness. A live five-object evaluation meets the
structural targets, and the remaining gaps are traced to a documented
limitation of the local model rather than to the design. Closing them, and
completing the reference-based and teacher-rater evaluation, is the immediate
next step.
